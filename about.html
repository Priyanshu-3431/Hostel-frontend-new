About College Hostel

Our college hostel provides a safe, comfortable and disciplined living environment for students from all branches. With modern rooms, nutritious mess food, round-the-clock security and dedicated student support, we aim to make hostel life as smooth as academic life.

Facilities

Everything you need, on campus.

🏢

Hostel Facilities

24/7 power backup, high-speed Wi-Fi, common recreation room, laundry service, and hot water supply.

🛏️

Room Facilities

Spacious rooms with study tables, wardrobes, ceiling fans, and comfortable beds for 2–3 students per room.

🍽️

Mess Facilities

Hygienic, nutritious meals three times a day with a rotating weekly menu and separate vegetarian options.

🛡️

Security

Gated entry with biometric/ID verification, CCTV surveillance, and on-site security staff round the clock.

🤝

Student Support

A dedicated warden and support desk to help with grievances, maintenance requests, and emergencies.

📋

Hostel Rules

Entry/exit curfew timings, visitor policy, ragging-free campus, and a code of conduct for a safe community.
